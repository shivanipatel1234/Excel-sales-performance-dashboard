**Excel Sales Performance Dashboard**
An interactive Excel sales performance dashboard designed to analyze sales, profit, products, regions, and monthly performance using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and KPIs.

**Project Overview**
This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, analysis, visualization, and dashboard design.

**Project Objectives**
Analyze overall sales and profit performance
Identify top and low-performing products
Compare regional sales performance
Analyze monthly sales trends
Calculate important business KPIs
Build an interactive sales dashboard
Generate business insights
**Tools & Techniques**

**Dataset**
The dataset contains 50 sales transactions with the following fields:
Order ID – Unique order identifier
Order Date – Date of the order
Customer – Customer name
Region – Sales region
City – Customer city
Product – Product sold
Category – Product category
Quantity – Number of units sold
Sales – Total sales value
Cost – Product cost
Shipping Cost – Shipping cost

**Calculated Fields**
Profit = Sales − Cost
Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Sales

** Key Business Insights**
Laptop generated the highest sales of ₹10,92,000 and the highest profit of ₹2,86,000.
Mouse generated the lowest sales of ₹1,30,000.
Laptop, Monitor, and Office Chair were the top three products by sales.
March recorded the highest monthly sales of ₹6,82,500.
